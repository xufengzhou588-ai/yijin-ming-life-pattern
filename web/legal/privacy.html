Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

MING is designed for cultural exploration and personal reflection. We use birth details and optional questions only to generate your reading and provide the features you request.

Information We Use

  • Birth date, birth time, birthplace, timezone, and optional true-solar-time settings for chart calculation.
  • Gender selection for traditional calculation policy. "Unknown" is supported.
  • Optional name or nickname for display only. It is not used in chart calculation.
  • Optional saved charts and oracle journal entries when you choose to store them on this device.
  • Subscription entitlement information when Pro is enabled through App Store purchase or restore.

How We Use Information

We use the information to generate structured readings, Pro reports, monthly or annual guidance, and guided I Ching oracle reflections. We do not use birth details or oracle questions for advertising or tracking.

Local Storage

Saved charts and oracle journal entries remain on your device or in your browser when you explicitly choose to save them. You can delete saved items inside the app.

Product Analytics

MING may use anonymous product-interaction events to understand onboarding completion, Today usage, Muyu Reset completion, Oracle saves, paywall views, purchases, and restores. These events must not include birth date, birth time, precise birthplace, name, raw Oracle questions, report text, or journal content.

Payments

The iOS version uses Apple StoreKit for subscription purchase, subscription management, refund requests, and restore. Apple may process payment and purchase information according to Apple's own privacy policies.

Your Choices

You can delete saved charts and Oracle journal entries inside the app. If a future account-sync feature is added, MING will provide account deletion and export instructions before that feature is released.

Professional Advice Boundary

MING does not provide medical, legal, financial, mental-health, safety, or emergency advice. Readings are not guarantees, diagnoses, investment instructions, or predictions of unavoidable outcomes.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact: support@mingpattern.app.
