Methodology & Data Sources

Last methodology review: August 21, 2026

MING uses a versioned rule system. Daily, monthly, and annual readings are generated from the user's birth input, chart structure, and date rhythm. They are not external rankings, live fortune feeds, or guaranteed predictions.

What Updates Automatically

  • Daily card and daily seal: generated each day from the user's chart and the current date rhythm.
  • Monthly compass: generated by month from chart structure and monthly stem-branch rhythm.
  • Annual rhythm: generated from annual stem-branch flow and the user's trait structure.

What Requires Manual Review

  • Rule tables for heavenly stems, earthly branches, five elements, ten-god relationships, and hidden stems.
  • Calendar dependency upgrades and solar-term calculation changes.
  • Location, timezone, longitude, and latitude dataset releases.
  • Paid report copy, localization, App Store metadata, privacy policy, terms, and support copy.

Important Boundary

MING is for cultural exploration and personal reflection. It does not provide medical, legal, financial, mental-health, safety, or emergency advice. Readings are pattern-based prompts, not guarantees or unavoidable outcomes.
