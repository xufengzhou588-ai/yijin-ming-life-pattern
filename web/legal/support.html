Contact

Subscription Help

If Pro access does not appear after purchase, use Restore Purchase in the app. Subscriptions are managed in your Apple account settings.

Data and Saved Readings

Device-only saved charts and oracle readings can be removed inside the app. If a future account-sync feature is added, we will provide account data deletion instructions in this support page.

Safety Boundary

MING cannot help with emergencies, medical diagnosis, legal matters, investments, or mental-health crisis support. Please contact qualified local services for those needs.
