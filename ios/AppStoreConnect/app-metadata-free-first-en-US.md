## Description

MING turns Chinese Four Pillars and I Ching-inspired structure into a modern self-reflection experience.

Enter your birth details to generate a structured life-pattern reading that explores how you tend to move, connect, build, decide, and respond to change. MING emphasizes traceable reasoning, plain-language interpretation, and practical reflection rather than fixed prediction.

Free launch features include:

- A deterministic Four Pillars blueprint
- Plain-language behavior dimensions with confidence and visible evidence
- Unknown and approximate birth-time handling
- A personalized Today focus derived from chart structure and the current date
- Five action seals: Focus, Spark, Ground, Connect, and Restore
- A 30- or 60-second mindful Muyu reset with optional sound and haptics
- One-small-action and evening reflection steps
- A seven-day trail and private local review history
- Clear boundaries around what the reading can and cannot decide for you

MING is for cultural exploration, journaling, and personal reflection. It is
not medical, legal, financial, mental-health, safety, or emergency advice, and
it does not promise future outcomes.
