# App Review Notes — Free First Release

MING's first App Store release is free. This build does not include active
In-App Purchase, external payment, subscriptions, Restore Purchase, or a test
unlock flow.

MING is an informational lifestyle app for cultural exploration and personal reflection. It translates Chinese Four Pillars and I Ching-inspired structures into modern language. It does not provide medical, legal, financial, mental-health, safety, or emergency advice. It does not promise outcomes or make fixed predictions.

Reviewer instructions:

1. Open the app.
2. Complete onboarding and generate a Blueprint with birth date, time, and
   place. Unknown or approximate birth time is supported.
3. In Blueprint, inspect the plain-language behavior dimensions, confidence,
   calculated Four Pillars, and evidence summaries.
4. Open Today. Review the personalized focus, choose one of five action seals,
   complete a 30- or 60-second Muyu reset, choose one small action, and record
   an evening mark. The seven-day trail is stored locally.
5. Confirm there are no luck points, leaderboards, auto-tapping, guaranteed
   outcomes, purchases, external payments, or test unlocks.
6. Use Trust to open Privacy Policy, Terms, Support, and Methodology.

Original-product clarification:

- MING is not built from a commercial template or app-generation service.
- Its deterministic calculation policy, true-solar-time evidence, ten behavior
  dimensions, daily loop, written interpretation, visual assets, and 104
  golden boundary fixtures were created specifically for this product.
- MING is unrelated to Couple Flight Chess, the other app on this account.
  The two apps use separate repositories, code stacks, assets, content, data
  models, and user workflows.
