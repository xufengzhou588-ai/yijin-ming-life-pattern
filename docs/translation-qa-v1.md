# Translation QA v1 — Chinese Pattern Language to US Product English

## Goal

MING should not feel like a literal Chinese metaphysics translation. The English version should feel like a trustworthy US-facing self-reflection product rooted in Eastern pattern language.

The translation target is:

> accurate meaning, natural English, culturally legible, App Store-safe.

## Core Positioning

Preferred English frame:

> Eastern pattern wisdom translated into modern self-understanding.

Avoid making the product sound like:

- a fortune-telling authority;
- a fixed fate prediction;
- medical, legal, financial, or mental-health advice;
- a generic astrology clone;
- a mystical black box.

## Translation Principles

1. Translate intent, not words.
   - Do not map every Chinese命理 term one-to-one.
   - First identify what the sentence is trying to help the user understand.

2. Keep agency with the user.
   - Use “suggests,” “may,” “tends to,” “watch,” “reflect,” “choose.”
   - Avoid “will,” “must,” “destined,” “certain,” “guaranteed.”

3. Replace fatalistic language with pattern language.
   - “运势” → rhythm, timing cue, pattern shift.
   - “财运” → money pattern, opportunity access, resource boundaries.
   - “婚姻” → relationship rhythm, communication pattern, commitment context.
   - “吉凶” → supportive signal / pressure signal / watch point.

4. Make Pro feel deeper, not more supernatural.
   - Pro should explain why, what to watch, and what to do next.
   - It should not promise more precise prediction.

5. Preserve Eastern aesthetics through tone and imagery.
   - Use calm, symbolic, pattern-based language.
   - Avoid overloading the interface with unexplained traditional terms.

## High-Risk Words

Avoid or heavily qualify:

- guarantee
- guaranteed
- destiny
- destined
- fate
- fatal
- bad luck
- good luck
- lucky / unlucky
- cure
- diagnose
- predict / prediction, except in disclaimers such as “not a fixed prediction”
- wealth forecast
- marriage forecast
- health forecast
- must
- definitely
- certain
- will happen

| --- | --- |
| 命 | life pattern / structure / rhythm |
| 八字结构 | Four Pillars structure / pattern structure |
| 运势 | rhythm / timing cue / pattern shift |
| 财运 | money pattern / opportunity access / resource boundaries |
| 官杀压力 | pressure, standards, responsibility, external demands |
| 桃花 / 婚姻 | relationship rhythm / attraction pattern / communication context |
| 吉凶 | supportive signal / pressure signal / watch point |
| 改命 | adjust choices / change behavior / create a new path |
| 断事 | read the pattern / identify a likely theme |

## Preferred Sentence Patterns

Use:

- “This pattern suggests...”
- “You may notice...”
- “A useful way to read this is...”
- “Use this as a timing cue, not a fixed outcome.”
- “The final choice remains yours.”
- “This is a reflection map, not a verdict.”

Avoid:

- “You will...”
- “Your destiny is...”
- “This proves...”
- “You must...”
- “This year is lucky/unlucky.”
- “You are guaranteed to...”

## Example Transformations

Bad literal translation:

> Your career driver is biased toward rules, and output expression is medium.

Better:

> You work best when expectations, roles, and standards are clear. Your ability becomes more visible when ideas are turned into concrete deliverables.

Bad literal translation:

> Your wealth luck will change in the next three years.

Better:

> The next three years may bring shifts in money and resource rhythm. Use them as timing cues for testing, advancing, and reviewing choices.

Bad literal translation:

> Your marriage has pressure.

Better:

> Relationship pressure may appear when expectations, responsibilities, or boundaries remain unspoken.

## App Store Safety Rule

Any claim that touches money, relationships, health, career, or future events should be framed as:

- reflective;
- probabilistic;
- non-diagnostic;
- non-guaranteed;
- user-controlled.

## QA Checklist

Before release, check every English string for:

- meaning preserved from Chinese;
- natural US product English;
- no absolute prediction;
- no professional-advice implication;
- user agency preserved;
- Pro value framed as depth and clarity, not supernatural certainty.
