# P2 Retention Loop Notes

Date: 2026-08-25

## Scope

- Today remains the default pane after a saved birth profile exists.
- The daily loop now has five concrete steps: daily focus, action seal, Muyu Reset, one small action, and evening mark.
- The five action seals are preserved as product labels: Focus, Spark, Ground, Connect, Restore.
- Daily records now store seal, intention, ritual completion, ritual duration, tap count, action choice, evening status, pause, repair, date pillar, relation, theme, and evidence context.
- Daily content is seeded from the long-term chart structure, current date pillar, and recent daily records.
- The previous percentage-style daily boost has been replaced with qualitative action tendency labels.

## Gentle Continuity

- The rhythm indicator can be hidden.
- Pause creates a protected daily record and does not show failure language.
- Skipped evenings are recorded as neutral history, not punishment.
- Repair is limited to two uses per month and targets only a missing or skipped previous day.
- Seven-day trail and monthly review read the same local daily records.

## Muyu Reset

- Free flow includes 30-second and 60-second duration choices.
- Users choose an intention before starting.
- The tap target uses a low-latency WebAudio wooden-fish timbre and light haptic feedback when available.
- Sound, haptics, and motion can be turned off.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables the ritual motion preference.
- Completion copy is "Intention set" and never converts merit, luck, auto-tapping, or leaderboards into a product mechanic.

## Open Verification Items

- Acoustic QA should still be done on device speakers and headphones before release.
- VoiceOver and real mobile browser testing should confirm the timer, tap target, and evening status controls are comfortable without visual context.
