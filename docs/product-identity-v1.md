# Product Identity v1

## Core Promise

This product is not another fortune feed.

It is a traceable Chinese life-pattern system that turns traditional
structures into modern decisions, real-world experiments, and personal
reflection.

## What Makes It Ours

1. Deterministic chart calculation comes before interpretation.
2. Conflicting signals remain separate dimensions instead of being hidden
   inside one dramatic verdict.
3. Every important interpretation can point back to structured evidence.
4. Time reports recommend experiments and boundaries, not guaranteed events.
5. Cultural symbols are used as attention anchors, not supernatural claims.
6. AI may explain existing conclusions but may not invent the underlying
   chart or overwrite confidence.

## Signature Experiences

### Life Pattern Ritual

The generation animation reveals the real calculation sequence: birthplace
and time, solar terms, pillars, elements, traits, and evidence.

### Monthly Compass

Each month provides:

- one relative structural focus;
- one boundary to protect;
- one real-world experiment;
- one month-end reflection question;
- one collectible Monthly Mark generated from the month's stem-branch
  structure.

The Monthly Mark is a cultural symbol and attention anchor. It does not claim
to change luck or produce guaranteed outcomes.

### Moment Oracle

The Moment Oracle uses six three-coin casts to create a primary hexagram,
changing lines, and a relating hexagram.

- Randomness selects coin faces only.
- Hexagram mapping and interpretation rules remain deterministic.
- The reading translates change into one current structure, one movement
  pattern, one direction, one action, and one boundary.
- Saved readings form a private device-only Oracle Journal.
- The same saved question should not be repeatedly recast within 24 hours.

The Oracle is a reflection tool, not a guarantee, professional Liu Yao claim,
or replacement for medical, legal, financial, or safety-critical advice.

### Western Interpretation Layer

For English-speaking users, modern behavior language comes first. Traditional
Chinese terms remain available in an optional `Chinese Roots` layer for users
who want cultural depth without needing prior metaphysics knowledge.

Humor may soften intimidating terminology, but it must point at the dramatic
wording, never at the user, their identity, or their chart. Mystery should
create curiosity; the explanation should still create clarity.

## Competitive Rule

Competitors may reveal a user need, but their feature implementation is not
our product specification.

Before adding a feature, answer:

1. What user problem does it solve?
2. What structured data makes our version defensible?
3. What behavior or decision changes after the user sees it?
4. Why would the feature still matter if the mystical decoration were
   removed?
5. Can the result be tested, traced, or reviewed?

If these questions have no strong answer, the feature should not enter the
roadmap.
