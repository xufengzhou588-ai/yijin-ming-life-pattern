# US Paid MVP - Two-Day Scope

## Goal

Deliver a web experience that can test whether US users understand the
product, feel personally recognized, and show intent to purchase a one-time
deep report.

This is a conversion-test MVP, not an App Store production release.

## Day 1 - Consumer Experience

- Replace engineering-first positioning with the working brand
  `MING · Life Pattern`.
- Lead the free result with a memorable behavioral archetype.
- Keep Four Pillars, evidence, and traditional terms available as deeper
  layers rather than the opening message.
- Add personalized previews to paid modules.
- Present a clearly labeled `$9.99` one-time complete-report test offer.
- Add birth-time accuracy and explain when hour-pillar conclusions are
  tentative.
- Add an initial United States location and IANA-timezone framework.

## Day 2 - Conversion and Trust

- Expand the career report to purchase-quality depth.
- Refine the locked preview and report-unlock flow.
- Add privacy, informational-use, and data-deletion language appropriate for
  external testing.
- Add lightweight local conversion events without collecting birth data.
- Test English copy, US timezones, DST dates, mobile layout, and locked versus
  unlocked states.

## Explicitly Out of Scope

- Real App Store payment or receipt validation.
- Production accounts and cloud sync.
- Complete global city coverage.
- App Store submission assets and legal review.
- Claims of scientific validation or guaranteed prediction.
- Large-scale professional chart calibration.

## Success Signals

- A new user can explain the product in one sentence after seeing the result.
- The free archetype feels specific enough to create curiosity.
- The paid preview makes the missing value obvious without hiding the entire
  product.
- Test users can state what they expect to receive for `$9.99`.
- No user mistakes the test unlock for a real purchase.
